2. TIMING-AWARE RESULTS

Benchmark   LLBP MPKI   LLBP-X MPKI   Reduction
Kafka       0.236015    0.225555      4.43%

3. AVERAGE RESULTS

Mean LLBP MPKI: 2.302247
Mean LLBP-X MPKI: 2.218297

Arithmetic mean of individual MPKI reductions: 3.89%
MPKI reduction calculated from mean MPKIs: 3.65%

4. ADDITIONAL EXPERIMENTS

Zero-latency models evaluated:
- tage64kscl
- llbp
- llbpx
- llbpx-threshold9

Measurement instructions: 100,000,000

Average LLBP-X improvement over LLBP:
3.66% (mean of per-benchmark percentage reductions)

The threshold-9 variant produced slightly higher MPKI
than the default LLBP-X on all three tested benchmarks.

5. EVALUATION LIMITATIONS

- Only 3 of the official 14 benchmarks were evaluated.
- Timing-aware and zero-latency experiments used
  different measurement lengths.
- Results should not be described as an exact
  reproduction of the complete paper evaluation.
- The official notebook uses TAGE-SC-L 64K as the
  reference predictor for its MPKI reduction graph.
- The official notebook's Mean row uses reduction
  calculated from mean MPKI values.

CONCLUSION

Across the three tested workloads, timing-aware
LLBP-X achieved lower ROI MPKI than timing-aware
LLBP. The average per-workload reduction was 3.89%,
while the reduction of mean MPKIs was 3.65%.

These results support the effectiveness of LLBP-X
on the evaluated workload subset.
